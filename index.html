Lulin Li

Ph.D. Student in Economics

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Economics at the University of Connecticut. My research interests are industrial organization and platform economics.

My work examines competition in content-creation markets and the effects of government subsidies on firm behavior in China’s electric vehicle market.

I hold an M.A. in Economics from Columbia University and a B.A. in Economics and Physics from Mount Holyoke College.
